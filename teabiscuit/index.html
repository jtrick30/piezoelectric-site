For adult children helping an aging parent

Know what the doctor said, and get it done.

Tea Biscuit turns your parent's doctor visit into a clear plan, with the reason for each step and one sitting to make the calls. Your parent stays in charge.

The visit is the easy part

Afterwards there's a referral to chase, a new prescription, a test to book, and clinics that don't talk to each other. Most families don't have a doctor to ask.

What you get

  • Your parent presses one button at the visit. Nobody has to take notes.
  • You both get a plan with the reason for each step, in plain words.
  • One sitting to make every call, with the numbers, scripts and forms ready.
  • You decide together who watches and who acts.
  • You can see where each step stands:
✓ Done◷ Waiting on clinic! Stuck

Why it's different

  • We get it done, not just tell you. Other apps stop at a summary. Tea Biscuit takes you through every call and form in one sitting, in the fastest order, and shows you what's still waiting.
  • Your data and your choices stay yours. We never sell your data, and no one pays us to send you to a service. You choose every provider; if your doctor named one, it shows first.
  • Built by a family physician. Built by a doctor who knows where plans fall through after the visit, and how to close those gaps.

How it works

  1. Set it up together with your parent, in about 15 minutes.
  2. Press record at the next doctor visit.
  3. Finish the plan in one sitting and see what's done.

For caregiver support organizations

Reach more families without adding staff.

Tea Biscuit helps your organization stay with older adults and their families between coach contacts, so they follow the doctor's plan and stay healthy at home.

More families, same team

Ontario's caregivers are expected to grow from 4.2 to 6.5 million by 2030. Between coach contacts, families carry the doctor's plan alone. In Ontario, only about half of patients are seen within a week after an emergency visit, and people with dementia less often. Timely follow-up is linked to lower mortality and fewer admissions.

What Tea Biscuit does

  • One button records the visit, with a spoken notice to the doctor.
  • The family gets a plan with the reason for each step.
  • One session where the family makes every call and fills every form.
  • Everyone sees where each step stands, and your coaches see only the stuck ones:
✓ Done◷ Waiting on clinic! Stuck

Why it's different

  • It gets the plan done, not just written down. Families finish every call and form in one sitting. Coaches see only the steps that are stuck.
  • Safe to recommend. No data sold, no referral fees, no steering. Families choose their own providers, and privacy is designed for Ontario rules.
  • Built by a family physician. Built by a doctor who knows where plans fall through between visits, and how to close those gaps.

How a pilot works

  1. A 20-minute call to see if it fits your families and your team.
  2. A fixed-fee pilot with a small group of early-stage families.
  3. A results report: plan steps completed, stuck steps resolved, caregiver confidence, and families supported per coach.

Where we are

A working prototype, tested with sample data only, and Ontario evidence on the follow-up gap. No pilot results yet. Measuring them with you is the point of the pilot.
