Education — prototype, open to collaborators

FieldQuote

Preceptors give feedback out loud all day, and most of it never makes it into a field note. FieldQuote records the post-encounter debrief and drafts structured field notes for the resident to review and the preceptor to sign off. It's built around the resident: the aim is to surface their learning needs across a shift and a rotation, then answer each one with targeted learning material in the way they learn best. If you're an educator or a learner tired of writing field notes, I'd like to hear from you.

Why "Piezoelectric"?

"Piezoelectric materials convert mechanical pressure into electrical power. Healthcare is under immense pressure. The question is whether we can transform that pressure into intelligent power that actually helps."

This site is about that conversion. Taking the pressures of modern medicine — burnout, access barriers, information overload, admin burden — and building AI that does something useful about them. Not hype. Not promises. Real work, carefully studied, honestly shared.
